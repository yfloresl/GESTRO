# GESTRO Usage Policies

**Version:** 2026-11-14.1 · They complement the Terms and the Privacy Policy of Kyros-Tech S.R.L. (Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia · administracion@kyros-tech.com).

## 1. Acceptable use
Use GESTRO only to run your restaurant. Do not upload unlawful, misleading or infringing content; do not ask diners for data you do not need; do not share your account; do not try to see other restaurants' data or bypass permissions. Breach may lead to limiting or suspending the account.

## 2. What each person can and cannot do
- **Owner/administrator:** configures the restaurant, staff, roles and prices, and sees full reports.
- **Staff:** access only what their role allows (kitchen sees tickets; the cashier takes payments; the warehouse manages inventory). The System checks permission on the server, not only on screen.
- **Diners and third parties:** see the menu, place orders and calls from their table's QR code, book through the web and see **their own data**. They cannot access the rest of the System and **never pay** the Provider.
- **Kyros-Tech platform team:** may enter in **read-only support mode**, recorded in the audit trail.

## 3. Payments, discounts and tips
The restaurant is responsible for what it charges. Discounts require a reason and have a cap; tips are not restaurant revenue and are recorded separately. Every cancellation keeps its reason, author and date.

## 4. Invoicing and taxes (SIN)
GESTRO **does not issue electronic invoices and is not connected to the SIN/SIAT**. The internal receipt is not a tax invoice. Tax obligations belong to the restaurant.

## 5. Artificial intelligence
If the assistant is enabled, its answers are indicative, may contain errors and are not professional advice. Do not enter data you do not want shared with the model provider. Important actions require a person's confirmation.

## 6. Files and images
Only JPG, PNG or WebP images are accepted; they are validated by content, stored in private per-branch space and are not public, except a dish photo on the public menu.

## 7. Retention and deactivation
See Privacy Policy sections 6 and 7. On cancellation you may request an export and deactivation of the account; deactivated data stops being visible and accessible and is kept only for the periods stated there.

## 8. Expired subscription
The System becomes **read-only**: you can view and pay; no new operations are recorded and no data is lost for that reason. Renewing before expiry adds to the current period.

## 9. Demo accounts
Demo accounts and data contain no real data and may be reset at any time.

## 10. Security and reporting problems
Do not test for vulnerabilities without written authorization. If you find a flaw, tell us at administracion@kyros-tech.com and do not disclose it before we can fix it.

## 11. Cookies
Only strictly necessary cookies and storage, with no advertising or third-party tracking (Privacy Policy section 5).
