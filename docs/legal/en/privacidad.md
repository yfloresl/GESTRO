## 1. Data we process
- **Account and staff:** name, contact email, role, branch and the Kyros ID identifier (`sub`). We do not store your password: Kyros ID handles it.
- **Restaurant:** legal name, tax ID (optional), menu, prices, photos, inventory, purchases, sales, cash, accounting and statistics.
- **Diners and third parties:** what the restaurant records or they enter: name and phone in a reservation, order notes, table number, uploaded receipts.
- **Technical:** audit logs (who did what and when), hashes of IP and browser as proof of acceptance, and minimal security data.

## 2. What we use it for
To provide the System, keep it secure, audit sensitive actions, give support, compute the restaurant's own statistics and meet legal obligations. **We do not sell data or use it for third-party advertising.**

## 3. Who owns the data
Operational data belongs to the restaurant (Terms §5). The Provider acts as a processor on the restaurant's behalf. The restaurant decides which diner data it collects and must inform them. Each restaurant only sees its own data: the database enforces per-branch isolation.

## 4. Who we share it with
Only with providers needed to operate (hosting, Kyros ID identity, email/notifications if enabled) and with authorities when required by law.
