## 1. Acceptable use
Use GESTRO only to manage your restaurant. Do not upload illegal, misleading or rights-infringing content; do not ask diners for data you do not need; do not share your account; do not try to see other restaurants' data or bypass permissions. Breach may lead to limiting or suspending the account.

## 2. What each person can and cannot do
- **Owner/administrator:** configures the restaurant, staff, roles and prices, and sees full reports.
- **Staff:** only access what their role allows (for example, kitchen sees tickets; cashier takes payments; warehouse manages inventory). Permissions are enforced on the server, not just on screen.
- **Diners and third parties:** see the menu, place orders and calls from their table QR, book online and see **their own data**. They have no access to the rest of the System and **never pay** the Provider.
- **Kyros-Tech platform team:** may enter **read-only support mode**, which is recorded in the audit log.

## 3. Invoicing and taxes (SIN)
GESTRO **does not issue electronic invoices or connect to SIN/SIAT**. The internal receipt is not a tax invoice. Tax obligations belong to the restaurant. TODO-VERIFY.

## 4. Artificial intelligence
If the assistant is enabled, its answers are indicative, may contain errors and are not professional advice. Do not enter data you do not want shared with the model provider. Important actions require a person's confirmation.

## 5. Files and images
Only JPG, PNG or WebP images are accepted; they are validated by content, stored privately per branch and are not public except a dish photo on the public menu.

## 6. Retention and disabling
See Privacy Policy §6 and §7. **No data is deleted: it is disabled.** On cancellation you may request an export and for the account to be disabled; disabled data stops being visible and accessible, is kept for audits with no deletion date, and only an audit role can see it, with a stated reason and traceability (TODO-VERIFY).

## 7. Expired subscription
The System becomes **read-only**: you can view and pay; no new operations are recorded and no data is lost for that reason.

## 8. Demonstration accounts
Demo accounts and data are read-only, contain no real data and may be reset at any time.
