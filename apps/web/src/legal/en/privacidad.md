# GESTRO Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2026-11-14.1 · **Data controller:** Kyros-Tech S.R.L. (the "Provider"), Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia · **Privacy contact:** administracion@kyros-tech.com

## 1. What data we process
- **Account and staff:** name, contact email, role, branch and the Kyros ID identifier (`sub`). We do not store your password: Kyros ID handles it.
- **Restaurant:** legal name, the restaurant's tax ID (optional, entered by the restaurant itself), menu, prices, photos, inventory, purchases, sales, cash register, accounting and statistics.
- **Diners and third parties:** whatever the restaurant records or they type: name and phone on a reservation, order notes, table number, uploaded receipts.
- **Technical:** audit logs (who did what and when), hashes of IP and browser as evidence of acceptance, and minimal security data.

## 2. What we use it for
To provide the System, keep it secure, audit sensitive actions, give support, compute the restaurant's own statistics and meet legal obligations. **We do not sell data or use it for third-party advertising.**

## 3. Who owns the data
Operational data belongs to the restaurant (Terms section 5). The Provider acts as a processor on the restaurant's behalf. The restaurant decides what diner data it collects and must inform them. Each restaurant sees only its own data: the database enforces isolation per branch.

## 4. Who it is shared with
Only with providers needed to operate (infrastructure, Kyros ID identity, notifications if enabled) and with authorities when the law requires. Artificial-intelligence features, if the restaurant enables them, send only the necessary context to the model provider.

## 5. Cookies and local storage
We use only what is **strictly necessary**: a session cookie (`httpOnly`) to keep you signed in and local storage for your theme preference and temporary screen data. **We use no advertising or third-party tracking cookies.** You can clear them in your browser; you will have to sign in again.

## 6. How long we keep it
- **Tax and accounting information** (sales, receipts, journal entries, purchases): **no less than 8 years**, because of the restaurant's tax and commercial obligations.
- **Contact messages** to the Provider: 2 years.
- **Everything else** (account, staff, reservations, orders): the time needed for the purpose and for applicable legal obligations.
When an account ends, or on request, data is **deactivated** (logical archiving): it stops being accessible and visible to the restaurant, its staff and other users, and is kept only for the periods above; only an audit role may see it, with a stated reason and traceability. Once the periods lapse and no legal obligation prevents it, data is deleted or anonymized. Backups rotate in cycles.

## 7. Your rights
You may request **access**, **rectification**, **deactivation** or **objection** regarding your data, and an exportable copy. If you are a diner, your request is handled through the restaurant and, if needed, with the Provider's help. Write to administracion@kyros-tech.com; we will verify your identity and **reply within 15 business days**. The Political Constitution of the State recognizes the right to privacy (art. 21) and the privacy protection action (art. 130). As of today Bolivia has no comprehensive personal data protection law; this policy follows that constitutional framework, Law 164 (General Telecommunications, Information and Communication Technologies Law) and Law 453 (rights of users and consumers), and will be adjusted when a specific law exists.

## 8. Security
Encrypted secrets (AES-256-GCM), per-branch data isolation in the database, role-based access, audit of sensitive actions, private files validated by content, and encrypted communications. No system is infallible; tell us if you detect a problem.

## 9. Minors
GESTRO is intended for people **aged 18 or over** and for businesses. We do not intentionally ask for minors' data; if a restaurant records it (for example a minor on a reservation) it is responsible for having the guardian's authorization.

## 10. Changes and contact
This policy is versioned; if it changes materially you will be asked to accept the new version. Contact: Kyros-Tech S.R.L., Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia · administracion@kyros-tech.com.
