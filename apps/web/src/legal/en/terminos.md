## 1. What GESTRO is and its scope
GESTRO is an online service (SaaS) for restaurants: menu and photos, floor and QR tables, orders, kitchen, cash register, inventory and recipes, purchasing, reservations, receivables, staff and shifts, basic accounting and statistics. Available modules depend on the plan.

- **It is not an e-invoicing service and does not connect to Bolivia's SIN/SIAT.** Issuing tax invoices is outside GESTRO's scope; the document it issues is an internal receipt. TODO-VERIFY with a tax advisor.
- **It does not replace an accountant, lawyer or tax advisor.** Accounting, reports and the AI assistant are aids; the restaurant must review them before relying on them.
- It does not guarantee business results or that forecasts or suggestions will come true.
- It does not process diners' payments: QR or transfer payments are made by the restaurant with its own means and the System only records them.

Access uses a Kyros ID account. Each person is responsible for their account. The restaurant owner or administrator decides the roles of their staff (waiter, kitchen, cashier, warehouse, accountant, etc.) and is responsible for how they use the System. Credentials must not be shared.

The restaurant may export or request deletion of its data as described in the Privacy Policy. The restaurant is responsible for having a legitimate basis to load personal data of its staff and diners.

## 6. Diners and third parties
Diners and third parties (anyone scanning a QR, booking a table or viewing the menu) **never pay the Provider and do not sign this agreement**; the restaurant serves them and decides what data it asks for. The System always shows them their own data and orders.
